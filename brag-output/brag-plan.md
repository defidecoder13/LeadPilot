# /brag Plan: LeadPilot

## Planning Rubric

1. **What is it?**  
   LeadPilot is an AI-driven B2B lead discovery and sales outreach pipeline that converts simple business search queries into enriched intelligence, verified contacts, and personalized outbound campaigns.

2. **Who is it for, and what does it do for them?**  
   For founders, agencies, growth marketers, and sales leaders who need qualified high-intent leads without burning hours manually scraping directories and drafting outreach emails.

3. **What sets it apart?**  
   Unlike raw scrapers or static databases, LeadPilot executes an autonomous multi-stage workflow: automated discovery → comprehensive company intelligence & differentiators → verified email extraction → contextualized AI email drafting.

4. **What's the most impressive claim?**  
   From search query to verified decision-maker and customized outreach in seconds.

5. **What's the visual hook?**  
   A high-energy typography statement kicking off over the signature LeadPilot crimson and slate canvas: *"Stop scraping dead lists. Put your lead pipeline on autopilot."*

6. **What real UI or flow should be shown?**  
   - Discovery Request Form with live input simulation (`"B2B SaaS Agencies"`, `"London, UK"`, `"50 leads"`).
   - 5-stage pipeline tracker: `Research ✓` → `Contact ✓` → `Outreach ●` → `Approval` → `Send`.
   - Rich Company Intelligence Card with real extracted services, audience, and outreach hooks.
   - Contact email discovery card (`Found from website: alex@growthscale.io`).
   - Personalized Outreach email composer with AI insight chips and instant approval.
   - LeadPilot logo mark and clean payoff outro.

7. **What tone fits?**  
   `polished` — crisp, authoritative, high-energy SaaS motion design.

8. **What's the one-line share caption?**  
   Stop scraping dead lists. LeadPilot turns any business search into deep company intelligence, verified emails, and personalized outreach on autopilot.
